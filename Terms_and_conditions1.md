## Use of this application

This application is an educational market-research tool. It displays historical prices, calculated risk and return measures, a rule-based screen, and a historical portfolio simulation. It does not provide investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice.

## Data and results

Market data is requested from Yahoo Finance's public chart endpoint. Data may be delayed, incomplete, revised, unavailable, or subject to provider restrictions. The app does not guarantee accuracy or continuous access. Users should verify information independently.

Screen labels are based on fixed historical thresholds. They are not forecasts, personalized recommendations, or evidence that an asset is suitable. The portfolio simulation uses adjusted prices, does not periodically rebalance, and excludes fees, taxes, and foreign-exchange conversion.

## No trading service

The application does not connect to a brokerage, accept orders, execute trades, or hold assets. Users are responsible for decisions made using any displayed information.
