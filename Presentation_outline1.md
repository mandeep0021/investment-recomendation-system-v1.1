# Presentation Outline

The editable slide deck is generated as `Presentation1.pptx` by running `python Generate_deliverables1.py`.

1. **Title** - Investment Recommendation System.
2. **Problem and objectives** - consistent historical return and risk comparisons.
3. **Practical scope** - ticker-based historical research, optional benchmark, portfolio backtest.
4. **Architecture** - Streamlit interface, Yahoo chart endpoint, analytics, tests, and exports.
5. **Data retrieval and reliability** - supported ticker formats, caching, explicit errors, no synthetic fallback.
6. **Performance measures** - total and annualized return, volatility, Sharpe assumption, and drawdown.
7. **Historical-momentum screen** - transparent Buy/Hold/Sell thresholds and caveats.
8. **Portfolio backtest** - adjusted-price buy-and-hold total-return simulation, normalized weights, currency constraint.
9. **Dashboard demonstration** - market comparison, benchmark, asset detail, privacy and terms pages, exports.
10. **Testing and limitations** - mocked provider tests, costs and FX exclusions, no example market values.
11. **Conclusion and local preview** - run instructions and pre-launch requirements.
